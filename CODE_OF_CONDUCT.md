# Kairo community code of conduct

Everyone should be able to contribute without harassment or exclusion. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## How we work together

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Assume good intent while listening when someone describes an impact.
- Respect privacy and ask before sharing another person's private information.
- Help newcomers understand the project and its expectations.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's **Report content** option for abusive content. For project-specific concerns, contact [im-divxnshh](https://github.com/im-divxnshh) through an available private contact method listed on their profile. If none is listed, use GitHub's reporting process; do not post private incident details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a change in behavior, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants depending on severity and repetition. Reports should be handled with discretion. Maintainers are expected to follow the same conduct standards.
